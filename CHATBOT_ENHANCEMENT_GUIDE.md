## 📞 HUMAN HANDOFF

### **When to Escalate:**
- Complex complaints requiring human empathy
- Technical issues beyond chatbot capabilities
- Special requests requiring creative solutions
- Customer explicitly requesting human assistance

### **Handoff Process:**
1. Chatbot identifies need for human assistance
2. Collects customer information and issue details
3. Creates ticket in support system
4. Notifies customer of expected response time
5. Transfers conversation context to human agent
